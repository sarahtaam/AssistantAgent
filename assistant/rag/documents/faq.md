# NovaTel Billing & Account Knowledge Base

## What is an eSIM
An eSIM is a SIM built directly into the device, with no physical card.
Benefits: remote activation, switching carriers without swapping a card.
NovaTel offers postpaid eSIM contracts billed monthly. Activation happens
through the app or at an authorized store.

## How collections scoring works
Every client is scored from 0 to 100 (higher is better) based on payment
history and current unpaid balance.
- Score >= 85 (EXCELLENT): up to 4 monthly installments, no upfront payment.
- Score 65-84 (RELIABLE): up to 3 monthly installments, no upfront payment.
- Score 45-64 (AVERAGE): up to 2 installments, 20% upfront required.
- Score 25-44 (RISK): 2 installments within 7 days, 30% upfront required.
- Score < 25 (CRITICAL): full payment required within 48 hours.

## The collections process
1. Invoice becomes overdue -> a reminder is sent based on the client's tolerance tier.
2. Tolerance exceeded -> the line is suspended.
3. Grace period: 7 days after suspension to regularize the balance.
4. No payment received -> the line is permanently deactivated.
5. The line is automatically reactivated once payment is received.

## Payment plans
Clients can request an installment plan matched to their risk score.
Installments are calculated automatically based on the amount owed.
An upfront payment may be required depending on the risk tier.
A confirmation summary is provided once the plan is confirmed.

## Booking an in-store appointment
To book an in-store appointment, clients should use the NovaTel app's
booking flow. Store staff can also book appointments directly for
walk-in clients who need in-person support.

## Support escalation
If a client reports a technical or payment issue more than once in the
same conversation, the assistant proactively offers to open a support
ticket rather than waiting to be asked. This applies to failed payments,
failed biometric verification, and general technical issues.
